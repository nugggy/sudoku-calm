Sticker Studio pictures

Every image in this folder was rendered by tools/sticker from 3D models made by Kenney (www.kenney.nl):
Furniture Kit, Food Kit, Nature Kit, Holiday Kit, Cube Pets, Fantasy Town Kit, Pirate Kit, Space Kit,
Car Kit, Graveyard Kit, Castle Kit, Train Kit, Survival Kit, Mini Market, Coaster Kit, Minigolf Kit,
Toy Car Kit, Mini Arcade, Mini Characters, Mini Dungeon, Platformer Kit, City Kit (Suburban) and Mini Skate.

The models are licensed Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal), public domain:
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The floors, walls, windows and wall decorations are drawn by the renderer for this app.
